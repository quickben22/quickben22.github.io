Prime
or Not

Swipe right for prime, left for not prime. Five seconds a card. How long can you last?

How to play

  • Read the number on the card.
  • Swipe right if it is prime, left if it is not.
  • Each correct answer adds a point and buys you a fresh timer.
  • The first wrong answer or timeout ends the run. Beat your best score.

Privacy

The game stores your scores only on your device. It shows a single banner ad on the retry screen and asks for consent where required.
