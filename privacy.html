Prime or Not

Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you install and play Prime or Not (package com.companyname.primeornot), an Android game published by an independent developer. In this policy, “we” and “us” refer to the developer of Prime or Not.

Short version: the game has no account, no sign-in, and no developer server. Your scores stay on your device. The only third-party data collection comes from the Google AdMob banner shown on the game-over screen, and we ask for consent where the law requires it.

1. Information stored on your device

Prime or Not stores your best score, today’s score, daily streak, and related game statistics locally using Android DataStore. This information is not sent to us and is removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

We do not ask for, and the game does not collect, your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location, health data, or payment information.

2. Advertising

Prime or Not uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show one anchored banner ad on the retry/game-over screen. The game does not request an ad while you are on the start screen or during a round.

When ads are requested, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • Device or other identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and similar device identifiers.
  • Approximate location inferred from your IP address (not precise GPS location; the game does not request location permission).
  • App interactions, such as ad requests, impressions, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic information, such as SDK performance, hang, and error data.

This data is used for advertising and marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. It is shared with Google and, through Google’s ad technology, with advertising partners. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you, and we do not sell personal information.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Google’s AdMob data disclosure describes the SDK’s data practices.

3. Consent and your choices

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), we use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask whether you consent to personalized advertising and related data use. If you decline, the game does not initialize the ads SDK for that session and no banner is requested.

You can change your choice at any time using the Privacy options button on the start screen when it is available. You can also:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your Android version).
  • Opt out of personalized advertising through your device’s ad settings.
  • Clear the app’s local data or uninstall the app to remove scores and local preferences.

If you are in the United States, Google’s UMP may also present state-specific privacy choices where required.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on your consent for personalized advertising and the associated collection and sharing described above. You can withdraw consent at any time through the Privacy options control. The limited processing needed to show a non-personalized ad and to prevent fraud may rely on legitimate interests or legal obligations, as described by Google.

5. Children

Prime or Not is a general-audience number game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game is not enrolled in Google Play’s Families programme, and the AdMob app is not tagged for child-directed treatment. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help you address it.

6. Retention

We do not operate a server and do not retain your personal data. Scores remain on your device until you clear them. Google retains advertising and diagnostic data according to its own policies and retention periods; see the Google links above.

7. Security

Network requests made by the Google Mobile Ads SDK use HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we keep the app’s data collection to the minimum needed to show ads and run the game.

8. Data deletion requests

Because we do not hold personal data on a server, there is no developer-side account or profile to delete. You can remove all locally stored game data through Android Settings → Apps → Prime or Not → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app. For data held by Google through advertising, use the Google controls linked above or contact us and we will point you to the right process.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app’s features or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change, and the current version will always be available at this URL.
